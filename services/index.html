Private planning systems

A planning tool built around your own ERP reports and business rules, behind a single sign-in. It reads your daily files, applies your rules and tells each owner what to expedite, follow up or order — with Excel exports.

Example: our BO Planning System reads the daily back-order, stock, transfer and assembly files, allocates stock by ETA through the bill of materials, writes the line remarks and lists actions by owner.

How custom work runs

  1. Share a sample. Send example files (dummy data is fine) and explain the rules you follow.
  2. We build and test. The tool is checked line by line against results your team produced by hand.
  3. You review. Differences are explained; rules are adjusted until the results match how you work.
  4. Use it and adjust. Rules live on a settings page you can change yourself as vendors, lead times or processes change.
